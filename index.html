Approach

Contemporary design, spatial experience and a sensitivity to material, context and identity. Clarity you can feel, not just see.

  • Architecture

    New houses, residential and commercial buildings, from first sketch to construction detail.

  • Interior architecture

    Rooms planned around how they are used: light, proportion, material and the objects that will live there.

  • Adaptive reuse

    Reinterpreting existing buildings and interiors, keeping what carries meaning and transforming the rest.

  • Concept & visualisation

    Concept creation, spatial planning and cinematic imagery, including generative image and film, to test an idea before it is built.

Studio

Delnaz Salehi

I am an architect with a strong interest in the relationship between people, space and the built environment. My approach combines contemporary design, spatial experience and a sensitivity to materiality, context and architectural identity.

With a background in both architectural practice and academic research, I explore how spaces influence human perception, behaviour and everyday life. My work spans architecture, interior design and spatial transformation, with a particular interest in adaptive reuse and the reinterpretation of existing environments.

I aim to create thoughtful, functional and distinctive spaces that balance aesthetic clarity with meaningful human experience.
